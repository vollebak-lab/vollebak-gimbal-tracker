VOLLEBAK GIMBAL - OPERATOR QUICK START
======================================

NORMAL START
1. Give the gimbal clear space and connect its external 12 V power.
2. Connect the Pi Ethernet cable, Logitech camera, and IMX636 event camera.
3. Power on the Raspberry Pi and wait 30 seconds.
4. Double-click "Vollebak Gimbal Dashboard" on the Windows desktop.
   If the shortcut is not installed yet, double-click INSTALL_DASHBOARD_SHORTCUT.cmd once.
5. Wait for http://192.168.0.3:8080 to open.
6. Confirm LOGITECH and IMX636 show ONLINE. The IMX636 can be plugged in later;
   its watchdog scans every three seconds and starts Bart's detector automatically.
7. Clear people and cables, then click START TRACKING.

NORMAL STOP
1. Double-click STOP_SYSTEM.cmd.
2. Wait for "gimbal parked."
3. Turn off servo power before handling the mechanism.

FIRST TIME ON A NEW WINDOWS USER OR FRESH CHECKOUT
1. Connect the hardware and Pi.
2. Double-click SETUP_HANDOFF.cmd.
3. Approve the Windows administrator prompt if shown.
4. Enter the Pi password when requested.
5. Use RUN_SYSTEM.cmd from then on.

The launcher always starts paused and centers the gimbal. It does not begin
tracking until the operator clicks START TRACKING.

If anything fails, read docs\HANDOFF.md. For an unsafe movement, disconnect the
physical servo power immediately; do not rely on software as an emergency stop.
